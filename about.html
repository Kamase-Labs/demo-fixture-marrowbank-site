About Marrowbank

Marrowbank Painting & Coatings Ltd. started in 2009 with one van, a compressor and a promise to show up when we said we would. We are still owner-run and still a crew of about twenty-two painters, apprentices and coatings technicians.

Dale Ferreira, owner. Journeyman painter, Red Seal. Signs every quote.
Priya Natarajan, estimator. Takes off every drawing and prices every job.
Callum Reyes, site supervisor. Runs our larger commercial and strata sites.
Mei-Lin Abara, office manager. Scheduling, invoices and WorkSafeBC paperwork.

Safety and insurance

Every worker is WorkSafeBC registered and orientation-trained. We carry $5,000,000 commercial general liability and hold a current COR certificate. Site-specific safety plans are written for every job over one week.
